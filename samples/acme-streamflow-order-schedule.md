# Acme StreamFlow Order Schedule

Version 1.1

Effective 15 February 2026

This fictional Order Schedule is governed by the Acme Cloud Master Agreement.
It orders the StreamFlow Cloud Service for Customer.

1. Subscription

Customer may allow up to fifty Authorised Users to access StreamFlow during
the twelve-month Subscription Term.

Provider must make the production StreamFlow service available 99.9 percent of
each calendar month, excluding announced maintenance and events outside
Provider's reasonable control.

2. StreamFlow-Specific Restrictions

Notwithstanding the Affiliate permission in the Cloud Master Agreement,
Customer may not allocate StreamFlow access to an Affiliate unless that
Affiliate is named in this Order Schedule.

Customer must not use StreamFlow to provide a bureau service or outsourced
processing service to another party.

3. Support

Provider must respond to a severity-one support incident within one hour,
provided that Customer supplies reasonable diagnostic information.

4. Precedence

If there is a conflict or inconsistency concerning StreamFlow capacity or
permitted use, this Order Schedule prevails and controls over the Cloud Master
Agreement.
