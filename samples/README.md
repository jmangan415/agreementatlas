# Fictional agreement family

These documents describe an invented company and product. They were written
for the AgreementAtlas test suite and demonstration UI; they are not adapted from
a real customer or supplier agreement.

They are distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence. They are
examples only, not legal templates or legal advice.

Recommended upload order:

1. `acme-cloud-master-agreement.md`
2. `acme-streamflow-order-schedule.md`
3. `acme-data-processing-addendum.md`
4. `acme-insighthub-order-schedule.md`
5. `acme-streamflow-service-level-agreement.md`
6. `acme-cloud-master-amendment-1.md`

Together they demonstrate definitions, chapeau/list negation, general and
product-specific permissions, scoped precedence, conflicting definitions,
data-processing duties, SLA exclusions and a dated amendment chain.

`gold_questions.json` is the model-free acceptance set. It identifies the
controlling source/evidence expected for each demonstration question without
containing any real agreement text.
