# Acme Cloud Master Agreement

Version 1.0

Effective 1 January 2026

This fictional Cloud Master Agreement is between Acme Systems Ltd
("Provider") and the entity identified in an Order Schedule ("Customer").

1. Definitions

A. "Affiliate" means an entity that directly or indirectly controls, is
controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

B. "Authorised User" means an employee or contractor whom Customer permits to
access the Cloud Service on its behalf.

C. "Customer Data" means information submitted to the Cloud Service by or for
Customer.

2. Access Rights

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and
use the Cloud Service during the Subscription Term for Customer's internal
business operations.

Customer may permit its Affiliates and Authorised Users to access the Cloud
Service, provided that Customer remains responsible for their compliance with
this Agreement and unless an Order Schedule expressly prohibits that access.

Customer must not sublicense, resell or make the Cloud Service available to any
third party other than an Authorised User.

3. Customer Responsibilities

Customer must protect its account credentials and must promptly notify
Provider of suspected unauthorised access.

Customer must not use the Cloud Service to process unlawful content or to
infringe another person's intellectual property rights.

4. Fees and Renewal

Customer must pay undisputed invoices within thirty days of the invoice date.

Unless an Order Schedule states otherwise, a Subscription Term will renew for
successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives at least sixty days'
written notice before the current term ends.

5. Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other party's Confidential Information using at
least reasonable care and may disclose it only to personnel who need to know it
and are bound by confidentiality duties.

6. Order of Precedence

If there is a conflict or inconsistency, a Data Processing Addendum controls
for personal-data processing, then the applicable Order Schedule prevails for
the ordered Cloud Service, and this Agreement controls in all other cases.

7. Assignment

Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other party's
prior written consent, except that either party may assign it to an Affiliate
as part of a merger or sale of substantially all relevant assets.

8. Disclaimer

The Cloud Service may contain errors. Customer remains responsible for
decisions made using reports generated by the Cloud Service.

9. Technical Restrictions

Customer shall not: (a) share administrator credentials; (b) reverse engineer
the Cloud Service; or (c) permit Affiliates to access administrative functions
except as expressly stated in an Order Schedule.
