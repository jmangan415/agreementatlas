# Acme StreamFlow Service Level Agreement

Version 1.0

Effective 15 February 2026

This fictional Service Level Agreement applies to the StreamFlow Cloud Service
ordered under the Acme StreamFlow Order Schedule.

1. Availability Commitment

Provider must make the production StreamFlow service available 99.9 percent of
each calendar month.

2. Exclusions

Downtime caused by announced maintenance, Customer systems, Customer breach of
the Order Schedule, or events outside Provider's reasonable control is excluded
from the availability calculation.

Customer may not claim a service credit unless Customer submits a support
ticket within ten business days after the affected month.

3. Remedy

An approved service credit is Customer's sole monetary remedy for a failure to
meet the availability commitment.

4. Precedence

If there is a conflict concerning availability calculations or service
credits, this Service Level Agreement controls over the StreamFlow Order
Schedule.
