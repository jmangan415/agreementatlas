4. Commercial Terms

4.1 **Payment.** Licensee shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30)
days of the invoice date. Invoices are issued annually in advance, the first on
the Subscription Term Start.

4.2 **Taxes.** All fees are exclusive of VAT and of any other applicable sales,
use or withholding taxes, which are payable by Licensee in addition.

4.3 **Uplift.** The Annual Software Fee and the Premier Support fee increase by
three per cent (3%) on each anniversary of the Subscription Term Start. This
Section 4.3 replaces, for the Software licensed under this Transaction Document
only, the uplift provision at Section 6.4 of the Master Agreement.

4.4 **Renewal.** Notwithstanding Section 12.2 of the Master Agreement, the
Subscription Term under this Transaction Document does not renew automatically.
NDS shall issue a renewal quotation not less than one hundred and twenty (120)
days before the Subscription Term End.

4.5 **Price hold.** Licensee may purchase additional Software Licences for the
SKUs listed in Section 2 at the unit prices and discount levels shown, at any
time before 30 April 2028. Additional Software Licences purchased under this
Section 4.5 are co-terminous with the Subscription Term.

5. Special Provisions

The following provisions apply to the Software licensed under this Transaction
Document and, to the extent of any inconsistency, prevail over the Master
Agreement and over the Licence Model Schedule in accordance with Section 14.9
(Order of Precedence) of the Master Agreement.

5.1 **Definition of Authorised User for InsightHub.** For InsightHub only, and
notwithstanding Section 1(F) of the Licence Model Schedule and Section 1.4 of
the Master Agreement, "**Authorised User**" means a named individual employee of
Licensee or of a Licensee Affiliate listed in Schedule 1 to this Transaction
Document. Contractors, agency staff and third-party personnel are excluded from
this definition and must be licensed under the Contractor Named User Licence
Model.

5.2 **Affiliate allocation.** Licensee may allocate Software Licences under this
Transaction Document to the Affiliates listed in Schedule 1 only. Allocation to
any other Affiliate requires NDS's prior written consent, which shall not be
unreasonably withheld. For the avoidance of doubt, this Section 5.2 permits
allocation only, and does not permit assignment or transfer, which remains
governed by Section 14.3 of the Master Agreement.

5.3 **Contractor Named User cap disapplied.** Section 3.3(iv) of the Licence
Model Schedule, which caps Contractor Named User Software Licences at
twenty-five per cent (25%) of total Named User Software Licences, does not apply
to this Transaction Document.

5.4 **Audit frequency.** NDS may exercise its audit right under Section 9.1 of
the Master Agreement no more than once in any twenty-four (24) month period in
respect of the Software licensed under this Transaction Document, rather than
once in any twelve (12) month period.

5.5 **Occasional Named User day count.** For InsightHub only, the forty (40)
calendar day limit in Section 3.2(i) of the Licence Model Schedule is increased
to fifty-two (52) calendar days in any calendar year.

5.6 **Termination for convenience.** Licensee may terminate the VaultKeep
Archive licence at line 8 only, on ninety (90) days' written notice, effective
no earlier than the second anniversary of the Subscription Term Start. No refund
of prepaid fees is payable on such termination.
