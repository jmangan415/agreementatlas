# Acme InsightHub Order Schedule

Version 1.0

Effective 1 March 2026

This fictional Order Schedule is entered under the Acme Cloud Master Agreement
effective 1 January 2026. It orders the InsightHub Cloud Service for Customer.

1. Product Definition

"Authorised User" means a named Customer employee who has been assigned an
individual InsightHub account; contractors and Affiliates are excluded.

2. Subscription

Customer may allow up to twenty-five Authorised Users to access InsightHub
during the Subscription Term.

3. InsightHub-Specific Restrictions

Customer must not permit an Affiliate or contractor to access InsightHub
unless Acme Systems Ltd approves that person in a signed Order Schedule
amendment.

4. Precedence

If there is a conflict concerning InsightHub user eligibility or permitted
use, this Order Schedule prevails and controls over the Cloud Master Agreement.
