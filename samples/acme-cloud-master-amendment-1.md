# Acme Cloud Master Agreement Amendment No. 1

Effective 1 April 2026

This fictional Amendment is entered into by Acme Systems Ltd ("Provider") and
the entity identified in an Order Schedule ("Customer"). It amends the Acme
Cloud Master Agreement effective 1 January 2026.

1. Security Notice Amendment

Section 3 of the Acme Cloud Master Agreement is hereby deleted and replaced
with: "Customer must protect its account credentials using multi-factor
authentication and must notify Provider of suspected unauthorised access
within twenty-four hours."

2. Continuing Effect

Except as expressly amended above, the Cloud Master Agreement remains in full
force and effect.
